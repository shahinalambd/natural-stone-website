OUR STORY

Built on quality, trusted on landmarks

Natural Stone Co. LLC was established in 2000 to execute stone projects of any size. Today we are one of the most reputable stone supply and installation contractors in Oman and the UAE.

Our portfolio includes some of the Sultanate's most important buildings, including the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, the Royal Opera House, the Supreme Court building and Majlis Oman.

WHAT WE DO

Supply, fabrication and installation

Supply

Omani marble, imported granite, limestone, sandstone, slate, cobbles and pebbles, in blocks, slabs and tiles.

Fabrication

Cutting, finishing and waterjet work in our Samail Industrial City factory: polished, honed, leather, chiseled and more.

Installation

Experienced teams install floors, walls, facades, stairs and tops for residential, commercial and government projects.

QUALITY

Certified quality in every project

Our quality management is certified to international ISO standards. Every block and slab is checked for colour, thickness and finish before it leaves our factory.

That is why architects, consultants and main contractors trust us with their most demanding projects.
