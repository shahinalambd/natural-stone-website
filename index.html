ABOUT NATURAL STONE

The leaders in stone craft

Natural Stone Co. LLC is one of the most reputable stone supply and installation contractors in Oman and the UAE. Since 2000, we have delivered marble, granite, limestone and slate for projects of every size.

From our factory in Samail Industrial City, we cut, finish and install stone with the precision that national landmarks demand.

HOW WE WORK

From quarry to installation

One team handles every stage, so quality and timelines stay under control.

  1. Select

    We help you choose the right stone, colour and finish for your design and budget.

  2. Cut & finish

    Blocks are cut and finished in our Samail factory to your drawings and sizes.

  3. Deliver

    Material is packed and delivered to your site across Oman and the UAE.

  4. Install

    Our skilled installers fit every piece with care, from floors to facades.
